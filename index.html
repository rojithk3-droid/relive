How to play

Moving

WASD walk · Shift run · Space jump / swim up

F fly (Space up, C down)

Right-drag (or drag in Explore) to look · scroll to zoom · zoom all the way in for first person

V free camera: WASD fly the view, middle-drag pan, Q/E orbit (the House tool uses it automatically)

M map and teleport · P people · Tab hide the panel

Building

1–7 switch tools: Explore, Build, Select, House, Landscape, Nature, Land

R turn 90° · Q/E turn 15° · [ ] lift (Shift: 1 m) · G grid on/off

Select: G move · Del delete · Ctrl+D copy · Ctrl+Z undo

Walls snap to the edges of the 4 m grid; floors and roofs to its squares. Point at the top of a wall to start the next storey.

Living here

Enter chat · /w name msg whisper · /near msg nearby only · /me actions

/wave /cheer /clap /point /dance /sit · /tp name visit someone · /home

Claim up to 6 plots in Land. Nobody else can build or reshape your plots unless you open them.

The day/night cycle is shared: one day lasts 40 minutes.

ReLive needs its server

Open it by double-clicking Play ReLive.cmd in the relive folder, which starts the world server and opens http://localhost:8799/. Opening index.html directly from disk can't load the game.
